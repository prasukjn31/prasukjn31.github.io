01 / Customer shopping behaviorPostgreSQL · SQL · Power BI
01 — CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR

Does subscription status actually explain loyalty?

3,900 customer transactions were analyzed using SQL in PostgreSQL and presented in Power BI across demographics, categories, subscription status and purchase behavior.

Question
Which customer segments are driving revenue, and does subscription status line up with repeat behaviour?
Finding
3,116 of 3,900 customers were classified as loyal, while 72% of repeat buyers (5+ purchases) were still non-subscribers.
So what
The relationship between subscription and loyalty is weaker than the assumption suggests. That makes the subscription program a question to investigate, not an automatic success signal.

02 / AmEx consumer complaintsSQL · Power BI
02 — AMEX CONSUMER COMPLAINTS

Where does a low relief rate come from?

196,000+ CFPB complaint records were analyzed and narrowed to 13,665 relevant complaints across nine card issuers, including 6,166 credit-card-specific complaints, covering January 2025 to March 2026.

Question
How does American Express compare with other issuers, and which complaint issues appear most connected to the outcome?
Finding
1,484 unresolved purchase disputes represented 24% of AmEx credit-card complaints and were the largest complaint issue identified in the analysis.
Trend
The credit-card relief rate rose from 19.6% to 25.6% during late 2025 before falling again, a trend that warrants further investigation.
