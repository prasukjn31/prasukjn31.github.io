AmEx Consumer Complaint Analysis

Dataset196,000+ CFPB complaint records analyzed, narrowed to 13,665 relevant complaints across 9 card issuers (6,166 credit-card-specific), Jan 2025–Mar 2026.
Insight1,484 unresolved purchase disputes — 24% of AmEx's credit card complaints — emerged as the top driver of its low relief rate.
TrendRelief rate climbed from 19.6% (Aug 2025) to 25.6% (Nov 2025), then fell back to 21.8% by March 2026 — an unexplained swing worth investigating.
22.88%relief rate — 6th of 9 issuers
1,484unresolved disputes — 24% of CC complaints
1.01%untimely rate — 2nd worst of 9
Impact: matching Wells Fargo's relief rate (30.3%) would mean roughly 455 more customers resolved every year — a 32% improvement, moving AmEx from 6th to 4th of 9 issuers.
